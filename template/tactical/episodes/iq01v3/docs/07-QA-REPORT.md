# 07 · QA report: before (V2) / after (V3), quality gates and the QC loop

## What was really checked, and how
- **Frames:** contact sheets at 1 fps of both hooks, key stills, and frame-by-frame grabs around the freeze and the loop end. Claude inspected these images.
- **Audio:** measured only (ebur128, a 300 Hz–6 kHz "phone speaker" band, silencedetect, Scribe transcript). **No one listened.** Claude cannot hear audio.
- **Playback:** the exported MP4s decode from start to finish (ffmpeg full decode in the QC scripts). They were not watched in a player by a human.
