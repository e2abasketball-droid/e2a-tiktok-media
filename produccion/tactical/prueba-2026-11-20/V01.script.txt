# E2A-TACTICAL-2026-11-20-V01 — Hammer screen (frozen script)
HOOK: Watch the corner, not the ball.
SETUP: The hammer screen. A baseline drive, and a screen on the weak side, set on the help defender.
CASE A: If the corner defender leaves his man to stop the drive, the hammer pins him inside. Skip pass. Open three.
CASE B: If he stays home, nobody helps. The baseline is open: finish at the rim.
TAKEAWAY: The low man has to choose. Help, and you give up the three. Stay, and you give up the layup.
CTA: Which would you take? Follow for the next play.
