HOOK: He left before the screen. Who's open?
SETUP: The big comes up to set the ball screen. His defender jumps out to hedge before it even lands.
CASE A: So the screener slips. Plant, open up, dive to the rim. Over the top, catch in stride, layup.
CASE B: Same start. Now he waits. The screen is real, the big drops, so the guard stops and pulls up.
TAKEAWAY: He shows early? Slip it. He waits? Use the screen.
CTA: You're guarding the screener. Show early, or wait?
