# E2A music v2

This replaces the single background track (`assets/music_v3.mp3`) with a library of 12 original
sports instrumentals in six styles. Each video gets its own arrangement: the music is cut to the exact
length of the video, the drop lands on the reveal, and it ducks around the narration.

## Why the old music felt flat (measured on the 2026-11-03 videos)

- Every video used the same 45 s track on a loop.
- The first ~16 s of that track are almost silent: in the first 2 s the music sits about 49 dB under the voice.
- It played at a fixed 0.12 volume, so it never rose in pauses or on reveals.

## The new library (`library.json`)

| Style | Feel | Tempo | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hype` | NBA hype: half-time trap, brass stabs, heavy 808, crowd | 142-145 | records, highlights |
| `hiphop` | Basketball hip-hop: swung boom-bap, EP chords, bell hook | 90-92 | curiosities, tactics |
| `trap` | Epic sports trap: hat rolls, pluck hook, drops | 146-150 | spectacular plays, curiosities |
| `cinematic` | Taikos, string ostinato, braams | 120-124 | historic records, psychology |
| `drive` | High-energy sports beat: four-on-the-floor, arps, claps | 124-126 | tactical explanations |
| `suspense` | Ticking pulse and tension that explodes late into a hit | 98-100 | surprising stories |

Two tracks per style, each with its own key, tempo and hook. Rotation avoids the last 8 tracks used
(`history.json`), so consecutive Reels don't share a song.

Every track is generated by `e2a_music.py` from synthesis (no samples, no third-party recordings).
E2A owns the result: there is no licence to buy, nothing to credit and no AI or API credits spent.

Story types: `curiosity`, `record`, `highlight`, `tactical`, `surprise`, `psychology`.

## What each bed does

- **0 s:** an impact hit and the groove start together. There is no silent intro.
- **Hook and context:** a lighter groove. Tactical, trap and hype beds add a dribble and a sneaker squeak.
- **Build:** the last bar before the drop has a snare fill, a riser and a reverse cymbal.
- **Drop:** a bass hit and an impact land exactly on the first word of the reveal sentence. The drop
  is placed about 22% of the way into the narration; for suspense it comes at about 55%.
- **Break:** pad, claps and a riser just before the payoff sentence.
- **Second drop:** lands on the payoff sentence. Hype and trap beds add a crowd swell.
- **End:** when the narration ends the groove stops on a final chord hit, a bass hit and a net swish
  (plus a crowd cheer for hype, trap and cinematic). The reverb tail plays under the logo.

The mix is set around the voice:

- Kick and bass keep pushing under the narration, at -3 dB.
- Mids and highs duck by 11 dB while someone is talking and open up in pauses.
- A dip at 1.5-4 kHz leaves room for the voice.
- The level is computed from the actual `vo.mp3`. The voice is at least 17 dB above the music in the
  speech band (300-4000 Hz), and the music sits about 6-8 dB under the voice overall.

## Usage (per video)

```bash
python3 music/e2a_music.py --story curiosity --words template/src/words.json \
  --voice template/public/vo.mp3 --out template/public/music.mp3 --mix-json template/src/music-mix.json
```

`--mix-json` sets `premixed: true`, so `Main.tsx` plays the bed at volume 1, because the bed already
has its own fades and ducking. The default `music-mix.json` in the repo is
`{"premixed": false, "gain": 0.13}`, which is exactly the old behaviour. Until the automation runs
this command, nothing changes in production.

`make_sfx.py` exports the basketball one-shots to `template/public/sfx/`: swish, squeak, bounce,
bass hit, big impact, crowd cheer, riser and reverse cymbal.

## A/B test (2026-11-03 videos 1, 2 and 5)

The narration was recovered from the published videos by subtracting the known old track, which
removes it by about 24 dB. The test then made two mixes from that same voice and the same picture:

- one with the old music, exactly as before (0.12, same fades);
- one with the new bed.

Both mixes went through the same final normalisation (-14 LUFS).

| | Old | New |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice over music, speech band | 21.5-22.8 dB | 17.5-22.1 dB (target ≥ 17) |
| Music under the voice, full band | -16 dB | -6 to -8 dB |
| Music in the first 2 s (vs voice) | -49 dB | -6 to -7 dB |
| Final loudness / true peak | -14.7 LUFS / -1.3 dB | -14.6 to -14.8 LUFS / -1.1 to -1.4 dB |

## Switching production on (only after approval)

1. In the daily task prompt, replace the "Audio mixing" section that points to `assets/music_v3.mp3`
   with the usage command above. Pass the story type for each slot:
   - video 1 and 3: `curiosity`, or `record`, `highlight` or `surprise` when the story is one;
   - video 2 and 4: `tactical`;
   - video 5: `psychology`.
2. Commit `music/history.json` with each day's videos so the rotation carries over.
3. Move `assets/music_v3.mp3` to `assets/music/legacy/`. It stays as a backup and is excluded from
   selection (see `library.json` → `legacy`).

## Cinematic library (default since v6)

The user picked the cinematic style (taiko, strings, brass) after an A/B test on the Miller Reel on 2026-11-03. Since then the default music bed comes from `music/cinematic/`. It holds 10 instrumental tracks made once with the E2A ElevenLabs Music account (eleven_music_v2_5). Eight tracks are 30 s and two are 45 s, for the 30–45 s tactical videos. There is no cost per video.

- `scripts/episode-music.mjs` calls `music/e2a_cinematic.py` for each episode. The script:
  - picks a track long enough for the video, preferring the story mood (record → heroic/triumphant, tactical → hybrid…) and never one of the last 3 used;
  - shifts it so the track's climax lands on the payoff cut;
  - ducks it around the voice and sets its level from vo.mp3 with the same code as the synth engine.
- The `music.hits` sent to the template are the real times of the track's drop and climax in the video.
- `music.engine: "synth"` forces the old procedural engine, and `music.cinematicTrack: "cine-03"` forces one track. When no track is long enough (over about 44.5 s), the procedural engine is used automatically.
- To add or replace tracks: drop the mp3 into `music/cinematic/`, run `python3 music/cinematic/analyse.py` (it measures duration, drop and climax), and set `mood` in `library.json`.
- Do not generate new tracks for each video: that costs credits and needs the user's approval.
