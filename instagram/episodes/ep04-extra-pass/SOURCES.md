# Ep04 — The Extra Pass: sources (2026-10-31)
Web-searched; consistent points used: after a drive-and-kick, a help defender must close out; an extra pass forces a second closeout that most defences struggle
with; the extra pass should lead to a better shot (never just to pass); read the closeout (long closeout → attack it; no help → just shoot).
- hooperuniversity.com "Why You Should Make the Extra Pass" (breakdowns/penetrate-pass-pass)
- coachingtoolbox.net (extra pass must lead to a better shot / better shooter); pgcbasketball.com "One Extra Pass: unselfishness, decision-making"
- thehoopsgeek.com "4 spot passing" (drive-and-kick then extra pass drill)
