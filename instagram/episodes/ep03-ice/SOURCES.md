# Ep03 — ICE (ball-screen defence): sources and verification (2026-10-30)
Web-searched; consistent across sources: ICE (a.k.a. Blue/Down) is mostly used against side/wing ball screens; the on-ball defender gets on the
high side of the ball handler so he cannot use the screen and is forced toward the baseline/sideline; the screener's defender sits between ball and basket
and makes the "Ice" call; off-ball defenders have a lighter job than in hedge/switch.
- breakthroughbasketball.com "Ball Screen Defense - Ice, Drop, and Switch"; hooperuniversity.com "How to Ice Ball Screens";
  coachingtoolbox.net "Attacking ICE"; slappinglass.substack.com "Attacking ICE Coverage: A Primer".
- Narration deliberately limited to those common points (pop → big recovers to shooter, reject → keep the angle).
