How do you get past a defender without dribbling around him? Two steps, in opposite directions. This is the Euro step.
Here is the set-up. You attack the basket from the wing, and a defender slides in front of you to cut off the lane. The straight line to the rim is gone.
Here is the key. Your first step moves the defender. Your second step goes the other way, and takes you around him. Pick up the ball, and you only have two steps, so make them count.
Option one. Beat the defender in front of you. Gather on the run. Step wide to the left, and sell it with your shoulders, so he slides with you. Then push hard to the right, and finish with the ball away from him. Coaching cue: the second step goes sideways, not forward.
Option two. A helper waits at the rim. Take the same two steps, and the helper has to move too. When he steps out to stop you, pass to your teammate under the basket for an easy finish. Coaching cue: keep your eyes up, and decide before the second step.
Two things to watch. If the defender does not follow your first step, finish straight through the gap. And if he stays tight, protect the ball with your outside hand, and keep it high.
One gather. Two steps. One direction he cannot guard.
